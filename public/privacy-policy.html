Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Buy and its property enquiry services collect, use, store and protect information when users interact with our Instagram, WhatsApp and customer relationship management systems.

Information we may collect

We may collect information that users voluntarily provide or that is made available through authorised Meta platform interactions, including Instagram username or platform identifier, comments or direct messages, property locality interests, WhatsApp phone number, name, enquiry details, budget, property preferences and related communication details.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to property enquiries, provide relevant property information, route enquiries to authorised sales or telecalling staff, maintain enquiry records in our CRM, follow up on requested property information and improve customer service.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate our systems, including Meta platforms, WhatsApp, GitHub, Cloudflare and other technical infrastructure providers, only as needed to provide and operate the service.

Data retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, business records, operational needs and applicable legal requirements.

Your choices and deletion requests

Users may request access, correction or deletion of their personal information. Data deletion instructions are available at our Data Deletion Instructions page.
