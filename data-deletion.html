User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 6, 2026

If you have interacted with Smart Buy through Instagram, WhatsApp or our property enquiry service and want your personal information deleted, you may submit a deletion request using the method below.

How to request deletion

Send a WhatsApp message to +91 81481 27587 with the words DATA DELETION REQUEST. Please include enough information for us to identify your enquiry, such as your Instagram username or the WhatsApp phone number used to contact us.

What we will delete

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymise personal information associated with the requester from our customer enquiry and CRM systems, except information that we are required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process valid deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you if additional information is required to verify the request.

Contact

For questions about a deletion request, contact us at +91 81481 27587.
